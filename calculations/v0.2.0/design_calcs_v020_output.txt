========================================================================================
5. Input transients and OVP response
========================================================================================
Hot-short interruption spike: 5.25 + 1.31 A x 1.12 Ohm = 6.71 V << 62 V abs max (PASS)
Hot-plug ringing (undamped worst, 2 x VIN) 10.5 V at IN: < 62 V; FET is OFF during plug-in
  (UVLO turn-on delay >= 250 us + dVdT ramp), so the ringing does not reach +5V_P.
Overvoltage arriving while ON: output follows input for <= 6 us -> abs max respected for
  input slew <= 29.5 V/ms (bench supplies and adapters ramp in ms; a fast step while ON is NOT covered).
Reverse polarity: TPS2660 blocks down to -60 V (VERIFIED p.19); C_IN rated 50 V -> board survives -50 .. +50 V at J1
  (limited by C_IN rating; DESIGN statement, NOT TESTED).
ESD at J1 (IEC 61000-4-2 8 kV contact, 150 pF): dV on C_IN = 1.2 uC / 1 uF = 1.2 V (ASSUMED model) -> no TVS needed.
