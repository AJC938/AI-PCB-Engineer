# PCB Layout — Rev A

| Item | Value (read back from the saved board) |
|---|---|
| Outline | 90.0 × 64.0 mm rectangle, 4 × M3 holes (Ø3.2 mm NPTH) 3.5 mm from each edge |
| Layers | 2 copper, FR-4 1.6 mm, 1 oz |
| Components | All on the top side: 52 assembled parts (44 SMT, 8 THT) + 3 fiducials, 2 solder jumpers, 4 holes (61 footprints) |
| Routing | 331 track segments (widths 0.25 / 0.3 / 0.4 / 0.5 mm), 57 vias 0.6/0.3 mm, all on 31 nets; 0 unconnected |
| Zones | **+3V3_P** 251.2 mm² solid (U1 heat spreader, top) · **EF_RTN** 38 mm² isolated island under U5 (top) · **GND** top fill 3419 mm² · **GND** bottom plane 5342 mm² |
| Rules | 0.20 mm clearance, 0.25 mm minimum track, 0.5 mm copper-to-edge, 0.30 mm zone clearance |
| Silkscreen | Top only: reference designators, full connector/GPIO pin legend, JP1/JP2 function, rule R-31, "REV A v1.0" |

## Placement (top view, origin top-left)
- **Left:** J1 power input (wire entries at the left edge) → U5 eFuse with its RTN island → U1 LDO in the centre of
  the +3V3_P copper area.
- **Centre-top:** LED1 (5 V, red) and LED2 (3.3 V, green).
- **Right edge:** J3 (ultrasonic), J4 (UART), J5 (I²C), J6 (GPIO), top to bottom, with U2/U3/U4 and their
  passives next to J3/J4.
- **Bottom edge:** J2 (ESP32 interface, 1×12).
- Test points TP1–TP8 near their circuits; fiducials FID1–FID3 in three corners of the component area.
