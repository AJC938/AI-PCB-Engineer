## 2. Final design
90.0 × 64.0 mm, 2 layers, FR-4 1.6 mm, 1 oz, top-side assembly (44 SMT + 8 THT), Rev A. TPS26600 eFuse input
(OVP / UVLO / reverse / current limit), TLV1117LV33 3.3 V peripheral LDO, SN74LVC1T45 + SN74LVC1G17 ultrasonic
conditioning, I²C / UART / GPIO headers. Architecture B: the ESP32 DevKit stays on its own USB, and 3V3_ESP is
used only as a logic reference.

## 3. Engineering audit (`FINAL_ENGINEERING_AUDIT.md`)
- **TPS26600:** every pin checked against SLVSDG2G Rev. G: PASS. MODE→RTN = current limit with auto-retry.
  PowerPAD on an isolated RTN island: PASS. Accepted deviation: no RTN vias (19 mW normal dissipation).
- **Reverse polarity:** every conductive path traced. Protected for floating sources. **Not protected** when an
  earthed supply negative and an earthed USB host or peripheral form a loop through the common GND. Classified as
  inherent; documented with a procedural mitigation (L-1).
- **Thermal:** 251.2 mm² solid +3V3_P copper confirmed by board read-back → Tj ≈ 94.7 °C at 50 °C (CALCULATED).
  New published rating: 250 mA at ≤ 50 °C.
- **Signals, power domains, connectors:** PASS. J6 and the J2 signals are 3.3 V only (documented).
- **Footprints/mechanics:** 61 footprints reviewed. No absolute paths. KiCad 10 required. Mating housings,
  sensor pin order and enclosure are not verified.
- **Correction needed:** none electrical. DDR-22 sets the revision label to Rev A (silkscreen and title block only).
